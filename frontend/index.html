SAFETY & PRIVACY

Anonymous doesn't mean unprotected.

Stage 2 stores account details and, only after permission, private coordinates for matching. Exact coordinates are never returned to other users. Live anonymous chat is available in this prototype. Reports and blocks are stored locally by the backend.

  • In a real release, age eligibility and abuse reporting must be implemented before public launch.
  • Exact location should never be shown to other users.
  • Users should be able to leave, block, and report at any time.
  • Meetups should require mutual consent and encourage public locations.
